## Lead handoff format

When a LEASING lead needs an agent, text the agent's line in this shape:

> Hi [Agent], new LEASING lead for [address] (about [rent], [beds/baths]).
> Customer phone: [number]. Came in on LEASING (909) 277-9818. [Language or
> other constraints]. They asked [what they want]. [Name and email if known,
> otherwise "No name or email yet"]. Please reach out when you can.

Send follow-up updates to the same thread as details come in, for example a
name, an email, or a rent correction.

## Standard replies in use

Rental inquiry greeting:

> Hi! Thank you for contacting Bright Path Property Management. This is a text
> line for rental inquiries. Which rental property are you interested in?
> Please send us the property address, or if there's something else you need
> let me know and I'd be happy to help!

Agent handoff to the customer:

> Great news! For [address], your leasing agent is [Agent]. You can reach them
> at [phone] or [email]. Here's the listing: [URL] [Agent] will be reaching out
> soon. Thank you for contacting Bright Path Property Management!

Auto acknowledgement, which does NOT count as a human reply:

> Hey! Thanks for your message, we'll get back to you shortly.
